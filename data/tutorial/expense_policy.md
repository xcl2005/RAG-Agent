# ExampleCorp Travel & Expense Policy

> Public tutorial fixture for this repository. It contains fictional company data only.

## 1. Meals

Employees may claim meal expenses incurred during approved business travel.

- A single meal up to CNY 200 does not require manager approval.
- A single meal above CNY 200 requires manager approval before reimbursement.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable unless an executive exception is documented.

## 2. Hotels

- Standard hotel reimbursement is capped at CNY 800 per night.
- A hotel above CNY 800 per night requires manager approval and a written reason.

## 3. Ground transportation

- Taxi, metro and ride-hailing expenses are reimbursable when they are necessary for business travel.
- A single ground-transport expense above CNY 300 requires manager approval.

## 4. Required evidence

Every claim must include a receipt or other valid proof of payment.
Claims without evidence should not be approved automatically.

## 5. Example scenarios

- A CNY 180 dinner with a receipt: no manager approval is required under the amount rule.
- A CNY 260 dinner with a receipt: manager approval is required.
- A CNY 920 hotel night: manager approval and a written reason are required.

## 6. Intentionally missing information

This document does **not** define:

- the reimbursement deadline after a trip;
- a special airfare approval threshold;
- a production-system SLA;
- any employee's real identity or personal information.

If a question asks for one of those facts, a grounded system should say that the document does not provide enough evidence instead of inventing an answer.
